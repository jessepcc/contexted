Contexted is an alpha test of the social value of AI memory: not your polished profile, but the living context your assistant accumulates over time. Because that memory keeps changing, the experiment changes with it.

To start, copy the provided prompt into a chat with ChatGPT or Claude, review the excerpt it returns, and paste it back here. Contexted reads it for recurring themes and tone, then discards the raw text. Words over faces. Warmth over performance. Context over coincidence.
